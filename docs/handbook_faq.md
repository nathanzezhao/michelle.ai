# Employee Handbook FAQ (Sample)

## Work hours

Core collaboration hours are **10:00–16:00** in your local timezone. Outside that window, async work is fine.

## Remote work

Full-time employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week**. Fully remote roles are marked in the job posting. Managers approve permanent remote exceptions.

## Time off

- Vacation: 20 days per year (accrues monthly).
- Sick leave: 10 days per year.
- Request time off in the HR portal at least 5 business days ahead when possible.

## Equipment

New hires receive a laptop within the first week. Request peripherals (monitor, keyboard) via the IT ticket form. Company equipment must be returned within 14 days of your last day.

## Learning budget

Each employee gets **$1,000 USD / year** for courses, books, or conferences. Submit receipts in the expense tool with category "Learning".
