# Refund Policy (Sample)

Last updated: June 2026

## Overview

Customers can request a refund within **30 days** of purchase for most products and subscriptions.

## Who is eligible

- Standard and Pro plan customers: full refund within 30 days if less than 20% of the monthly quota was used.
- Enterprise clients: refunds follow the signed MSA. Contact your account manager; typical window is 45 days.
- One-time purchases (templates, add-ons): 14-day refund if the digital file was not downloaded more than once.

## How to request a refund

1. Email billing@example.com with your order ID.
2. Or open Settings → Billing → Request refund in the app.
3. Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days back to the original payment method.

## What is not refundable

- Custom professional services already delivered.
- Third-party marketplace fees.
- Accounts closed for Terms of Service violations.

## Partial refunds

If more than 20% of a Pro quota was used, we may offer a **pro-rated** credit instead of a full cash refund.
